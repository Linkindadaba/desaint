## 2. Scope of Deliverables

### A. Core Platform & Multi-Channel Modules
1. **B2C Retail Storefront:** Public online catalog for parents, teachers, and students.
2. **B2B Wholesale Portal:** Secure bookshop & distributor portal with carton/bulk tiered pricing.
3. **Institutional School Procurement:** Formal proforma generator with GRA compliance and 3% Withholding Tax (WHT) auto-deductions under Act 896.
4. **Walk-in Counter POS:** Fast point-of-sale receipt terminal for your Sunyani store counter.
5. **School Book Customizer & Proofing Suite:**
   - Ruling selector (Exercise 40/60/80pg, Note 1/3, Pre-school grid/double line, Drawing/Graph).
   - School Crest & Artwork uploader enforcing your 5,000–10,000 MOQ.
   - Plate setup fee automation (Free above 1,000 copies).
   - Digital proof upload and school proprietor sign-off portal.

### B. "Managerial Aspect" — Executive Management ERP
*Dedicated management suite requested by Mr. Solomon:*
- **CEO Live Analytics Dashboard:** Real-time revenue, daily counter sales, top-selling items, and profit margin analysis.
- **School Credit & Receivables Ledger:** Automated tracking of which schools took books on credit, payment schedules, and outstanding balances.
- **Role-Based Staff Access:** Granular permissions for Cashier, Graphics Manager, Logistics Officer, and Super Admin (CEO).
- **Regional Waybill Tracking:** Bus terminal parcel tracking (VIP, OA, Imperial, Metro Mass) and campus van delivery logs.

### C. Digital Identity & Infrastructure
- **Domain Acquisition & Setup:** Registration and DNS configuration of `desaintstationeries.com`.
- **Corporate Email Configuration:** Official business emails (`sales@desaintstationeries.com`, `info@...`, `ceo@...`).
- **Payment Gateway Integration:** MTN Mobile Money & Telecel Cash automated checkout + Manual MoMo Till verification + Direct Bank details.
