## 3. Core Business Operations & Scope
Desaint Stationeries operates as a comprehensive one-stop educational supply partner with 5 integrated pillars:

1. **Sourcing & Importation Supply Chain:** Direct international factory sourcing (containers/consignments from China, India, etc.), customs clearance, and landed cost management for educational goods.
2. **Graphic Design & Creative Branding Studio:** In-house artwork design for schools (crests, cover illustrations, emblems) and commercial branding (corporate stationery, brochures, promotional merchandise).
3. **Wholesale & Retail Distribution:** Supplying bookshops, stationeries, schools, retailers, and walk-in consumers.
4. **Customization & Printing Production:** Branded exercise books, Note 1/3 books, exam materials, and institutional printing.
5. **Institutional & General Supplies:** Servicing basic schools, SHS, colleges, NGOs, corporate organizations, and government agencies.
6. **Regional Logistics & Delivery:** Sunyani hub delivering across Bono, Bono East, Ahafo, Ashanti, Central, and Western North regions.

### A. Product Categories
1. **Educational Materials:**
   - Exercise books, Note 1 books, Pre-school books, Composition books, Drawing & sketch books.
   - Mathematical materials, Scientific equipment/materials, Teaching & Learning Materials (TLMs).
   - General classroom and school supplies.
2. **General Stationery:**
   - Pens, pencils, markers, highlighters, erasers, sharpeners, rulers.

## 4. Key Digital Platform Requirements
To scale Desaint Stationeries' operations, the software solution should address:
1. **Multi-Channel Catalog:** Retail catalog + secure wholesale login with volume tier pricing.
2. **Custom Exercise Book Configurator / Request for Quote (RFQ):** Allow headmasters and school proprietors to submit school logo, book size, page count, and ruling specs.
3. **Local Payment Support:** Seamless integration with MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, direct Ghana bank transfers, and Paystack/Hubtel.
4. **Ghana Tax & Statutory Invoicing:** Proper GRA invoicing compliant with Act 896, withholding tax (WHT/WHVAT) tracking for institutional procurement.
5. **Distribution & Waybill Tracking:** Dispatch routing from Sunyani hub to regional bus parcel terminals (VIP, OA, etc.) and direct deliveries.
